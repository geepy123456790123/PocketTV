How setup works

1

Download PocketTV

Download the latest PocketTV.nro file from this page.

2

Copy to microSD

Place PocketTV.nro in the /switch folder on your microSD card.

3

Open full-app mode

Hold R while opening an installed game, then launch PocketTV from the Homebrew Menu.

4

Create your subscription

Stripe starts the 7-day trial and returns you to the PocketTV setup page.

5

Get one code

Your account gets a short personalized activation code.

6

Enter it in PocketTV

Open PocketTV, choose Premium, and enter the code. The guide and live TV channels activate automatically.

Questions

Do I pay today?

No. The first 7 days are free. Billing begins after the trial unless you cancel.

Where do I use my subscription?

Inside PocketTV on Nintendo Switch homebrew. Choose the Premium setup path and enter your activation code.

How do I install PocketTV?

Download the latest NRO, place it at /switch/PocketTV.nro on your microSD card, launch it once from hbmenu, then open TV Setup and choose Install Home Screen Forwarder.

Can I still bring my own playlist?

Yes. PocketTV keeps the bring-your-own M3U/EPG option separate from Premium.
